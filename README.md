# Loose Lips

Talk to anyone in Shadows of Doubt in your own words. Or shout at them.

Replies are generated by the [Player2](https://player2.game/) desktop app running on your
own machine, so there is no API key, no account, and nothing leaves your computer except
the conversation itself going to the local app.

## What it does

Every citizen gains two new dialogue options:

* **Say something...** - type anything. Only people close by hear it.
* **Shout something...** - the same, but it carries into the next room and turns heads.

The citizen answers in character. What they say depends on their traits, their job, where
they live, how well they know you, how much they like you, how frightened they currently
are, whether either of you is holding a weapon, and what they actually witnessed.

They will not always tell you the truth. A nervous neighbour who likes the man you are
asking about will cover for him. Someone who despises him will tell you more than you
asked for. The killer will lie to your face.

Talk well enough and it changes the world. People hand over what they are carrying, open
doors, run, fight, give themselves up, call the police down on you, call them off you, or
point them at somebody else standing right there.

## Requirements

* Shadows of Doubt with **BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP)** - the bleeding-edge build, which is what
  Thunderstore Mod Manager installs by default.
* The **Player2 desktop app**, installed and running. The mod finds it at
  `http://localhost:4315`. If it is not running, the new options still appear but the
  citizen will tell you they have nothing to say.

## Installing

Through Thunderstore Mod Manager, or by hand: drop the `LooseLips` folder into
`BepInEx/plugins/` so you end up with `BepInEx/plugins/LooseLips/LooseLips.dll`.

## Configuration

Press **F4** in game to open the settings window. Everything can be changed from there
while you play, and it takes effect immediately - useful for the settings that only make
sense standing in the world, like voice reach, which shows you live how many people your
speaking and shouting currently reach. The Connection tab has a **Test connection** button
that pings the Player2 app and tells you what it found.

The same settings also live in `BepInEx/config/dev.hubert.looselips.cfg`, and appear in
BepInExConfigManager if you have it installed. All three are the same values.

The ones worth knowing about:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Base URL` | `http://localhost:4315` | Where the Player2 app is listening. |
| `Chat endpoint path` | `/v1/chat/completions` | Correct this if your Player2 build differs - check `http://localhost:4315/docs`. |
| `Speak replies aloud` | off | Sends replies to Player2's text to speech. Leave off if you use Babbler. |
| `Talking radius` / `Shouting radius` | 6m / 22m | How far your voice carries. |
| `Use vanilla lines as influence` | on | Feeds the game's scripted answer to the model as tone guidance rather than showing it. |
| `Enable world effects` | on | Master switch. Turn off to make conversations purely cosmetic. |
| `Maximum like shift per line` | 0.15 | Caps how far one sentence can move a relationship. |

## How it works

Shadows of Doubt stores dialogue as a three-layer system called DDS: a tree of messages,
each message a list of blocks, each block a GUID whose text is looked up in
`dds.blocks.csv` at load time. Blocks themselves hold no text.

That last detail is what makes this mod possible without touching a single game file. The
lookup table, `Strings.stringTable`, is an ordinary public dictionary, and
`SpeechController` has an overload that speaks a dictionary entry by key rather than a DDS
GUID. So a generated line is registered in memory a moment before it is spoken, and from
the game's point of view it is just another line of dialogue - it gets a bubble, a
duration, subtitles, and it is overheard by whoever is nearby.

The model never touches the game directly. It returns a small JSON object proposing what
to say and what should happen, and `WorldEffectExecutor` checks every proposal against
real state before anything runs. A citizen cannot hand over an item they are not holding,
cannot summon police when no officer is within earshot, and cannot shift a relationship
further than the configured cap. A model that hallucinates gets a citizen who talks a big
game and does nothing.

Nothing is written to disk and nothing enters your save.

## Known limits

* Generation takes a second or two and the game does not pause, so the citizen shows a
  thinking beat while the reply is on its way.
* Item handover is limited to what a citizen is visibly holding.
* The typing box is drawn by the mod rather than using the game's own UI.
